## Why I mention it

Because it explains where everything else comes from.

In a mathematics olympiad there is no formula to apply. You are given a problem that resembles none you have seen and you have to build the path. It is a very different feeling from an exam, and it hooked me: I qualified among the five best students of Almería for the Andalusian stage.

That led me to choose Mathematics, then Computer Science, and to always look for the point where the two meet.

## The other two things from that period

**Baccalaureate with Matrícula de Honor**, with 10 out of 10 in every subject. I mention it once and do not repeat it anywhere else: it is a sign of persistence more than of talent, and in the portfolio of a professional with experience it counts for little. But it is there, because it completes the picture.

**A month-long academic exchange in Boston**, at 17. It was the first time I had to understand and make myself understood in English daily for weeks. I put it in relation to the C2: the certificate came later, but the confidence to speak arrived in Boston.
