## Why I mention it

In a mathematics olympiad there is no formula to apply. You are given a problem that resembles none you have seen and you have to build the path. It is a very different feeling from an exam, and I loved it from the first time I experienced it.

That eventually led me to study Mathematics and to keep looking for its connection with Computer Science.

## The other two things from that period

**Baccalaureate with Matrícula de Honor**, with 10 out of 10 in every subject.

**A month-long academic exchange in Boston**, at 17. It was the first time I had to understand and make myself understood in English daily for weeks.
